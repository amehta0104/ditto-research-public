### Pillar 1: Banking & Payments

- **ECB's Frank Elderson urges euro-area banks to prepare quickly for AI-assisted cyberattacks — interview published 13 May 2026 in the ECB Supervision Newsletter** — Elderson, Vice-Chair of the ECB's Supervisory Board, identified Anthropic's restricted-access Mythos model as a step-change in AI-assisted vulnerability discovery. The largest US banks have early access and are patching at compressed cycles; euro-area banks do not. Elderson's message: "Lack of access is not an excuse for inaction. On the contrary, it makes it even more critical that banks step up and act now," with explicit reference to DORA-style operational resilience expectations applied to ICT vulnerability remediation cadence. Banks and their contractors must accelerate patching of vulnerabilities that have historically been deferred to longer software update cycles. Practical significance for an identity/threat-defence vendor: this is the clearest supervisory statement to date that AI cyber resilience is now part of the SSM supervisory dialogue, not a 2027 problem. Identity-adjacent attack surfaces (mobile banking app integrity, authentication chains, KYC document submission paths) are exactly the kind of "minor vulnerability" stacks that Elderson is asking banks to compress patching cycles on.

Angle: this is the most honest available account of what mandatory identity verification costs when the infrastructure underneath it isn't ready — and the regulator's answer was not to relax the check but to hold the line and let the operational pain force the engineering. That is the argument every compliance-versus-conversion debate needs: **friction is not an argument against the control, it is an argument against your implementation of it.** The uncomfortable half — which a credible post must include — is that the people running it are asking for two more years.

Account:. Angle: the EUDI deadline was supposed to produce twenty-seven national ecosystems. With four months to run it is instead producing a short list of suppliers who can actually ship an interoperable wallet, and member states are buying from the same small pool. **The buyer question that follows is not "is your wallet compliant" but "how many other people's wallets does your vendor also run, and what happens to your sovereignty story when the answer is four."** That question is sharper for a European buyer than any feature comparison — and it pairs directly with the tech-sovereignty finding below.

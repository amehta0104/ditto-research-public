## Override-worthy this cycle

1. **Optery "Data Behind the Deception" 2026 Enterprise Social Engineering Survey — IT and identity-and-access-management staff targeted in 80.5% of organisations vs. 42.3% for executives; data brokers now the #1 attacker intelligence source; secondary coverage in window (Identity Week 18 May, Biometric Update 19 May; underlying report 12 May)** — A 421-respondent survey of cybersecurity professionals (conducted by TrendCandy on behalf of Optery) finds that attackers have re-targeted social engineering away from C-suite executives and toward the workforce that *operates the identity layer*: IT/IAM teams (80.5% of organisations), HR (44.7%), finance (43.9%), help desk (33%). The "intelligence supply" driving the shift is commercial data-broker aggregation — structured, searchable employee data including contact details, employment context and personal relationships — and government advisories plus leaked ransomware-group comms (e.g. Black Basta using ZoomInfo and RocketReach) corroborate the pattern. Angle: this is the operational evidence under the standing FCA-Rathi / ECB-Elderson "seams between firms" thesis (05-14 and 05-18 overrides) — the seam attackers are now exploiting is *the identity workforce itself*. Identity orchestration, threat defence and verification aren't just three procurement lanes — they're the three layers an attacker has to traverse once they're inside the help-desk-and-IAM team's inbox. Use the 80.5% / 42.3% asymmetry as the lead stat (it's fresh, citable, and reframes "attack surface" from a person to a *function*).

### Pillar 6: Identity verification (IDV)

- **Identy.io targets 1bn biometric verifications in Nigeria; on-device biometrics positioned as the structural answer to SIM-swap and SS7 OTP-interception attacks on banking — Biometric Update 19 May 2026** — Identy.io CEO Jesús Aragon (interviewed by Biometric Update in window) said the company aims to complete one billion identity verifications in Nigeria, framing on-device biometric authentication (fingerprint + facial, with liveness, capture and matching local-to-device, no centralised processing required) as the structural answer to Nigeria's banking-fraud surge — SMS OTPs are interceptable via SIM-swap and SS7 attacks; tying authentication to the user's own device and biometrics eliminates that class of attack. Do not name Identy.io in a post (treat as competitor-adjacent / not on competitors.md but in IDV lane); use as category-pattern only.

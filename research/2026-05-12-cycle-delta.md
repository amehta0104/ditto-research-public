### Pillar 4: KYC / AML compliance

- **AMLA launches public consultation on Home-Host Supervisory Cooperation RTS — 11 May 2026** — Draft RTS under Article 46(4) of the AML Directive (Directive (EU) 2024/1640) sets out duties of home and host supervisors and modalities of cooperation between them for cross-border groups of obliged entities. AMLA describes the framework as "simple, proportionate and efficient." Public hearing scheduled 28 May 2026, 14:00–16:00 CET. This is the fourth RTS package AMLA has opened (after the CDD, business-relationships/linked-transactions and pecuniary-sanctions consultations that ran February–May 2026). The CDD and Article 19(9) consultations closed 8 May; final draft to the Commission by 10 July. The Home-Host RTS does not set substantive CDD obligations — it defines the supervisory plumbing — but it shapes how single-rulebook CDD will actually be enforced across cross-border groups, which is the operational question every cross-border bank or fintech in scope is asking. ISDA filed its response to the AMLA CDD RTS the same day (11 May), an early indicator of how derivatives-trading obliged entities will push back on the scope of "occasional transactions" and electronic identification means.

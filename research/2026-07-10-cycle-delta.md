## New findings

### Pillar 1: Banking & Payments
- **AMLA 23 RTS/ITS package submitted to European Commission (July 10, 2026)** — Today is the statutory deadline by which AMLA must deliver all 23 Level 2/3 measures to the European Commission under the AML Regulation. The package covers three domains: (1) obliged-entity obligations — 8 mandates including RTS on standard/simplified/enhanced CDD (AMLR Art. 28(1)), business-relationship identification, linked-transaction criteria, group-wide AML programme requirements, and third-country high-risk country countermeasures; (2) supervisory processes — 10 mandates including AMLD6 Art. 53 supervisory methodology, risk-based supervision RTS, and the peer-review framework; (3) FIU processes — 5 mandates. Once adopted by the Commission (process typically takes 3–6 months), the measures apply directly across all 27 member states from 10 July 2027 with no national transposition. This is the first time a single EU authority has defined, in binding and directly applicable detail, what "know your customer" means for every regulated entity — bank, crypto-asset service provider, payment institution, and professional firm — across the bloc. For Ditto: the CDD RTS (Art. 28(1)) is the document that will specify exactly which identity attributes must be collected and verified for standard, simplified, and enhanced CDD, which is the regulatory foundation on which every European KYC technology stack will be built or rebuilt from mid-2027.

### Pillar 7: Identity ecosystem
- **Fragomen and SICPA launch global joint venture to build government-grade digital identity platform — announced July 8, 2026** — Fragomen (the world's largest global immigration law firm, with 6,000+ professionals in 70+ offices) and SICPA (the Swiss sovereign security company that secures the majority of the world's banknotes and passports, founded 1927) announced the formation of a new global joint venture headquartered in Switzerland. The JV's stated purpose: a standards-based platform delivering identity verification, verifiable credentials, document authentication, digital wallets, and credential lifecycle management in a single integrated ecosystem for governments, enterprises, and individuals. The partnership combines Fragomen's legal expertise in global workforce mobility and compliance with SICPA's credentialing infrastructure and secure sovereign digital technology stack. For Ditto: this is a high-signal market move — SICPA's government-grade provenance technology (the same trust chain used for banknotes) being combined with the dominant global immigration compliance firm to enter the digital identity space. The JV is targeting the same relying-party and credential lifecycle problem that Ditto's architecture addresses: who issues the identity claim, how is it cryptographically bound to the person, and how does a relying party verify it without re-collecting raw PII. The JV's entry also signals that the government digital identity market is large enough to attract major incumbent security infrastructure players, not just pure-play IDV startups.

- **Nigeria NIMC crosses 136 million NIN enrolments as NIMC Act 2026 takes effect — ward-by-ward national campaign launched (July 7–8, 2026)** — NIMC announced on July 7-8 that NIN (National Identification Number) enrolment has exceeded 136 million — up from 123.9 million in October 2025, adding more than 12 million people in nine months. The milestone coincides with the new NIMC Act 2026 (signed June 26 by President Tinubu), which replaces the 2007 Act, establishes NIMC as Nigeria's root certifying authority for digital ID infrastructure, embeds the NIN as the country's single foundational identity under a "one person, one identity" policy, and adds explicit provisions on digital credentials, cybersecurity, and data protection. NIMC has simultaneously launched a ward-by-ward enrolment campaign across all 8,809 political wards to reach women, children, people with disabilities, rural dwellers, refugees, legal residents, and Nigerians abroad. Compulsory data-protection training was mandated for all Front End Partners in July 2026. For Ditto: Nigeria's trajectory — 136M enrolled on a foundational layer, a new legal framework establishing NIN as the cryptographic anchor, and a regulatory mandate to extend coverage to the most excluded populations — mirrors the journey the EU is undertaking with the EUDI wallet but at a different maturity point. The convergence of foundational-ID scale with a new PKI and digital credentials mandate is the global pattern: sovereign identity infrastructure matures into a verifiable credential layer, creating demand for the selective-disclosure and privacy-preserving verification stack Ditto provides.

# Cycle Delta — 2026-07-23

Window: 2026-07-21 → 2026-07-23 (last 48 hours — Thursday run). This is a **genuinely active cycle** whose spine is a single theme printing from two directions in the same 48 hours: **the EU/European age-verification and digital-ID agenda is hitting friction on both the implementation side and the legitimacy side at once.** On July 21 **France became the first EU country to pass a blanket under-15 social-media ban — but the final text stripped out the clause that would have required platforms to build Arcom-approved age-verification systems**, leaving a law that mandates an outcome while deleting the mechanism. One day later (July 22) the **European Commission concluded eligibility checks and registered a European Citizens' Initiative — "Stop Killing The Internet: No Digital ID & No Age Verification"** — a formal, million-signature campaign to force EU digital-identity and age-assurance systems to stay *voluntary, privacy-preserving and non-discriminatory.* Read together they are the sharpest public signal yet that the binding constraint on the age-verification/EUDI push is no longer the technology or the deadline — it is a *trust-and-mechanism* gap, and the design that bridges it (selective-disclosure / ZKP age proofs that verify a threshold without surveillance) is exactly Ditto's thesis. The same window produced two more consolidation datapoints (**SwiftConnect acquires HID SAFE**; **OneSpan launches its unified DigipassONE platform**) that extend the "buyers want platforms, not point solutions" wave, plus a Web3 **ZK compliance SDK** print. Banking-reg, AML, fraud, mobile and IDV produced no new discrete in-window print.

### Pillar 11: Age assurance & privacy attributes
- **France becomes the first EU country to pass a blanket under-15 social-media ban (21 Jul 2026) — but the final text strips out the clause requiring platforms to build Arcom-approved age-verification systems, leaving a mandate with no prescribed mechanism** — Both chambers approved the ban (National Assembly 279–81); it prohibits under-15s from holding accounts on covered platforms (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, etc.). Crucially, lawmakers **removed** the provisions that would have (a) required platforms to implement age-verification systems approved by audiovisual regulator Arcom and (b) given Arcom power to impose compliance measures on platforms that failed to submit proposals. Implementation: new accounts subject to age verification from **1 Sep 2026**; existing accounts to be verified and under-age accounts suspended from **1 Jan 2027**. For Ditto: this is a textbook "outcome mandated, mechanism deleted" gap — the law creates a legal obligation to age-gate while leaving the *how* politically unresolved, which is precisely the vacuum a privacy-preserving age-proof (selective disclosure / ZKP threshold proof) is designed to fill. It also raises the stakes on the "highly effective age assurance" bar the UK OSA already enforces (Ofcom, 16 Jul). Strong POV fuel; pairs with the EU Citizens' Initiative (P3) as the two-sided friction story. Policy datapoint — do not name a vendor.

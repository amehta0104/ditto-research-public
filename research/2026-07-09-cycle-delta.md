- **Aduna, with AT&T, T-Mobile and Verizon, launches nationwide network-based Number Verification as a possession-based alternative to SMS one-time passwords (announced July 8, 2026)** — The three largest US carriers, via Aduna's Number Verification API, confirm in near-real-time that a mobile number is bound to the SIM and device presenting it, at the network routing layer — no SMS code, no shared secret. ### Pillar 11: Age assurance & privacy attributes
- **Scandit enters biometrics with on-device facial age-estimation for retail self-checkout — first biometric product from the smart-data-capture vendor (announced July 8, 2026)** — Scandit's Age Verified Self-Checkout: a shopper scanning an age-restricted item (alcohol, tobacco, medication) is shown a QR code, opens an age-check page, takes a selfie, and on-device vision AI estimates age instantly; above threshold, checkout continues; below, the shopper is prompted to scan an ID, read on-device and matched against the selfie to catch borrowed IDs. Scandit reports the flow completes in under 10 seconds (6× faster), auto-resolves ~80% of age checks, needs no additional kiosk hardware, and runs **100% on-device with no biometric or ID data transmitted or stored** by Scandit or the retailer. For Ditto: age assurance is a Ditto pillar (gambling/gaming age gates), and this is a fresh datapoint that facial age-estimation with a privacy-by-design, on-device, no-retention architecture is going mainstream in a new vertical (retail self-checkout) — the same privacy-preserving-attribute pattern (prove a threshold, don't expose or store the underlying data) that ZKP/selective-disclosure promises. Retail is adjacency, not Ditto's ICP, but the estimation-plus-ID-fallback + on-device-no-storage design is the reference pattern for age assurance under privacy scrutiny. Ecosystem datapoint — do not name a vendor. Hard anchor unchanged: **Ofcom statutory age-assurance effectiveness report due by end-July 2026** (first-year OSA report; will set the public compliance baseline).

### Pillar 6: LATAM

- **Brazil Drex privacy bill passes first committee: Chamber of Deputies approves anti-surveillance provisions for future CBDC (June 9, 2026).** — A committee in Brazil's lower house of Congress approved legislation that would establish privacy guardrails on any future official digital currency, including Drex. Key provisions: (1) government banned from tracking citizens' transactions without a court order; (2) no one can be compelled to abandon paper cash; (3) mandatory external security reviews; (4) regular public transparency reports. The bill must still pass additional committees, both chambers, and win presidential sign-off, so provisions remain subject to change. Ditto significance: the bill's design mirrors the "verify without exposing" architecture Ditto advocates — if enacted, the BCB could not access transaction data at will, requiring any identity/KYC compliance layer to prove regulatory adherence without handing over raw transaction visibility. This constraint would make ZKP-compatible KYC tooling not just preferable but structurally necessary for Drex-integrated financial institutions.

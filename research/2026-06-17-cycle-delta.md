Binance filed with Greece in January 2026 after failing to secure licenses in traditional EU crypto hubs (Germany, Netherlands). If the rejection is formalized before July 1, Binance would be barred from operating across all 27 EU member states once the MiCA grandfathering period expires. Competitors with confirmed MiCA authorization (Coinbase, Kraken, OKX, KuCoin) stand to absorb Binance's estimated ~18.5 million EU users. Neither HCMC nor ESMA has officially confirmed the rejection; Binance disputes the reports and says it has met all MiCA requirements.

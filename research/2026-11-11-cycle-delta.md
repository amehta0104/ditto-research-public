### Pillar 7: Identity ecosystem

- **Australia opens myID to private sector December 1 — Accenture AU$36.2M ATO contract and December/January fee structure confirmed** — The Australian Taxation Office awarded Accenture a AU$36.2M one-year contract for its Digital Identity Services branch, which runs myID (the government's Digital ID app). The contract was competitively tendered with 132 invited suppliers. From December 1, 2026, accredited private-sector services and identity providers can apply to the Digital ID Regulator to join the Australian Digital ID System; businesses pay for myID use from January 1, 2027; individuals remain free. The AU$654.3M (≈ US$474M) federal budget commitment from the 2026–27 budget underpins the rollout. Australia's Digital ID Act bars organisations from requiring a Digital ID (voluntary use). The Government's listed examples — opening a bank account, applying for a phone contract, signing a lease — signal that financial services onboarding is a primary target use case. Significance: Australia becomes the second major economy to operationalise a "government as identity infrastructure for the private sector" model (alongside the EU's EUDI wallet) with a hard December 1 go-live. Useful anchor's "government as identity layer" framing; directly parallels the Israel draft strategy (covered Nov 10 delta).

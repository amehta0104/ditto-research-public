### Pillar 3: Fraud / Deepfakes

- **Seqrite (Quick Heal Technologies) publishes Indian financial-institution deepfake fraud report — 18 May 2026** — Seqrite's enterprise security arm found that AI-driven impersonation attacks using synthetic voice, video, and identity manipulation are increasingly bypassing traditional verification mechanisms in India's banking sector: attackers mimic executives, relationship managers, or customers with high accuracy, enabling fraudulent authorisations, account takeovers, and real-time payment manipulation. The report recorded 265.52 million detections across 8 million enterprise endpoints between October 2024 and September 2025 (505 detections per minute). Key recommendation: shift from static identity verification to dynamic, behaviour-led validation with multi-layered authentication and anomaly detection across transaction flows. Note on geographic fit: India is outside Ditto's primary ICP (EU, UK, LATAM); treat as a category-pattern signal only. The "dynamic, behaviour-led validation across transaction flows" framing reinforces the orchestration-layer argument across all Ditto geographies.

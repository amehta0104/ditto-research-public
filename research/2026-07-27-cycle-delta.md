- **FCC advisory on bank impersonation scams; FTC data show $3.5B in US impostor-scam losses in 2025 (July 25, 2026 coverage)** — The Federal Communications Commission released a consumer advisory warning about bank impersonation scams; the American Bankers Association Banking Journal covered it July 25, 2026. Bank Policy Institute survey data (BPInsights Jul 25): the average number of bank impersonation scams per institution rose 150% from 2024 to 2025, with surveyed banks averaging 26,196 impersonation incidents in 2025. Scam mechanics: spoofed caller ID, fake fraud alerts, fake bank executive profiles. The identity defence layer needs to work inbound (who is contacting the customer posing as the bank) not only outbound (who is the customer claiming to be). US-centric but relevant framework for European social-engineering narrative.

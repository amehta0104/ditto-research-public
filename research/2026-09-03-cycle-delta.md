### Pillar 10: ZKPs in practice

- **ISO and IEC made the mobile driving licence and verifiable-credential standards free to download, removing a per-document paywall of up to $279 from the credential ecosystem (2026-09-02).** **ISO**, **IEC**, **Deutsches Institut für Normung (DIN)** and the **OpenWallet Foundation** announced at the **Global Digital Collaboration Conference 2026 in Geneva** that the **ISO/IEC 18013 series (mobile driving licences)** and the **ISO/IEC 23220 series (verifiable digital identity credentials)** are now available free of charge. Previously these documents cost roughly **$22 to $279 each**. DIN operates the distribution portal at **dinmedia.mdoc.online/en**, free to registered parties, **with copyright protections maintained**. The OpenWallet Foundation manages the initiative on a **sponsorship-based model**; sponsors listed include **A4 Advisory, AAMVA, Austroads, Explicit Selection, FaceTec, FAST, Fime, France Titres, iDAKTO, Idemia, Scytales and Stelau** ⚠️ (**do not enumerate the sponsor list in a post — name the standards bodies**). **Philippe Metzger**, IEC Secretary-General and CEO: the arrangement **"establishes a new model for how critical digital infrastructure standards can be shared globally."** **Daniel Goldscheider**, OpenWallet Foundation Executive Director: **"Making these standards available through a sponsorship-based model is a decisive step toward global interoperability."** Why it matters for Ditto: ISO/IEC 18013 is the standard the **EU ARF** builds mDL attestations on and the one **TSA's CAT-2 readers parse at 250+ US airports** (09-02 delta); **23220 is the generalised verifiable-credential family that sits underneath selective disclosure.** These are the specifications that make "prove the attribute, not the document" implementable rather than aspirational. **The POV writes itself and it is not a vendor pitch: interoperability had a price, and the parties least able to pay it were the small relying parties the EUDI wallet will need to accept credentials from December 2027. A pharmacy, a letting agent or a regional bank was never going to buy six standards documents to check an age claim.** Second-order point worth making carefully: **a sponsorship model means the funding for open infrastructure now comes from the vendors who sell into it — that is a good outcome and a governance question at the same time, and saying both is more credible than saying only the first.** ⚠️ **Constraint: free access is not the same as free implementation, and these standards are not ZKPs. 18013-5 provides selective attribute disclosure, not zero-knowledge proof. Do not conflate them.**

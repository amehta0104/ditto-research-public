## Override-worthy this cycle

1. **Microsoft Research unveils "Vega" — production-grade zero-knowledge proof framework explicitly engineered for the EUDI wallet, AI agents and on-chain verification — Microsoft Research blog, 22 May 2026** — Vega is a ZKP framework optimised for privacy-preserving digital credentials that lets a holder prove a single fact derived from a credential (over-18, citizenship, professional status) without revealing the credential itself or the underlying data. Headline benchmarks (a 1,920-byte credential): **212 ms proving time, 51 ms verification time, 150 kB proofs, 436 kB proving key** — i.e., mobile-hardware real-time. Joint work with Darya Kaviani, to be presented at the IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy in San Francisco. Microsoft names three explicit target use cases: the EU Digital Identity Wallet, AI agents acting on behalf of humans, and on-chain verification. Angle: this is the most consequential ZKP-in-production signal of 2026. The world's largest identity platform's research arm has published a benchmark-grade ZKP framework — with concrete latency numbers fast enough for live authentication — and *explicitly* designed it for the EUDI wallet ARF and the agentic-identity stack. Pairs directly with the still-pending OpenID Connect ASC 1.0 "Transformed Claims" vote (Pillar 10) and the 05-22 AuthZEN EIC award (Pillar 2): the protocol layer (ASC), the policy layer (AuthZEN) and now the cryptographic primitive layer (Vega) for privacy-preserving disclosure are all converging in the same six-week window. For Ditto, this is the strongest external validation moment of 2026 for the privacy-by-design ZKP architecture they sell — "Microsoft Research now ships the maths" beats any vendor claim.

2. **FBI Public Service Announcement: "Kali365" phishing-as-a-service kit hijacks Microsoft 365 OAuth tokens, bypasses MFA entirely — IC3 PSA I-052126-PSA, 21 May 2026; broad industry coverage 22–24 May 2026** — Kali365 is a Telegram-distributed PaaS toolkit, first observed April 2026, that uses *device-code phishing*: victims are tricked into completing a legitimate Microsoft OAuth device-code authentication flow, after which attackers harvest the issued access and refresh tokens. No credentials change hands; the MFA challenge is satisfied by the user. Once tokens are stolen the attacker reads Outlook, Teams and OneDrive without further authentication. Capabilities advertised: AI-generated phishing lures, automated campaign templates, real-time targeted-entity tracking dashboards, OAuth token capture. The FBI's recommended mitigations are organisational: disable device-code flow tenant-wide except where explicitly required by a business process. Angle: the OTP-phase-out story (05-21 Microsoft SMS sunset; UAE CBN mandate) was about *replacing* OTPs because they're phishable. Kali365 is the in-window evidence that *token-based MFA* is now being weaponised at production scale — the move to passkeys is the right move not because OTPs are bad but because *every form of MFA that issues a phishable token to a user-controlled flow* is in active exploitation. Cross-pillar: P9 passwordless (the architectural answer is phishing-resistant credentials bound to the device), P8 mobile/app security (device-code flows surface on mobile), P6 IDV (the identity-assurance signal at the start of the session is the only place to break the chain).

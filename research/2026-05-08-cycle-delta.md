### Pillar 7: Identity ecosystem

- **UK national digital ID and police-biometrics legislation in King's Speech, 13 May 2026 (announced 7 May 2026)** — Both bills among 12 priority areas the Labour government will outline at the State Opening of Parliament on 13 May. Voluntary digital ID with statutory anti-scope-creep protections; police-biometrics framework follows a separate consultation that closed February 2026 (results still unpublished). Sits alongside the just-closed (5 May) public consultation on the digital-ID scheme itself, and a separate "People's Panel" deliberative process running to 21 June. The UK approach contrasts deliberately with the EUDI mandate model: opt-in, no service conditioning, statutory limits before deployment. Identity-vendor relevance: a second large European market is now writing a digital-ID statute; the UK rulebook will not align with eIDAS2 by default, opening a near-term integration question for vendors selling into both markets.

- **IN Groupe "Digital Identity: Infrastructure That Changes Everything" white paper (published 7 May 2026)** — Benchmarks 210 countries/territories against a five-layer identity stack (identity register, credential issuance, authentication service, consent and data management, adoption ecosystem). Findings: only 33% have reached "leader" maturity; 36% are nascent (no functional system, no legal redress); 18 surveyed countries operate digital ID without a dedicated legal framework. Frames technology, governance, and ecosystem adoption as co-equal success factors — i.e. a country can have all three layers technically and still fail without a statute. Identity-vendor relevance: useful counter-narrative to "Europe is ahead, everyone else is catching up" — the data shows most of the world is structurally behind on the legal/governance layer, not the tech layer, which reframes where vendor expansion actually unlocks.
  - Source: https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/digital-id-success-at-scale-hinges-on-tech-governance-adoption-in-groupe
  - Date: 2026-05-07

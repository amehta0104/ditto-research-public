## Override-worthy this cycle

1. **Estonia's Information System Authority (RIA) launches €21.65M (≈$25.1M) procurement for its compliant EUDI Wallet — 96-month contract, applications due 29 June 2026 — Biometric Update, 21 May 2026** — RIA opened a competitive-dialogue tender covering software development, system integration *and* operating the wallet service for five years post-deployment, across the entire European Economic Area. RIA will invite 3-5 applicants to the second stage; the project is classified as "innovative strategic procurement." Head of the EUDI Wallet at RIA, Margit Aus: tenderers must deliver "a comprehensive solution… that enables users to securely store and present authentication data, use various types of attestation of attributes, and give digital signatures." Angle: this is the EUDI deadline turning from policy aspiration into a *priced, dated, multi-year procurement contract you can quote*. Among the standing high-preparedness member states (France / Germany / Austria / Estonia), Estonia is now the first to publish a single contract that bundles wallet build + 5-year operation in one envelope — i.e. the wallet is being procured as an *operated service*, not as a project. Pairs with the 05-21 Microsoft SMS-sunset override (consumer-scale OTP retirement) and the 05-20 Optery override (every centralised identity record is future attacker inventory): three signals in eight days that the identity stack regulated firms now have to integrate with is moving — wallet rails, passkey rails, data-minimisation rails — and orchestration is the only buying motion that absorbs all three at once.

2. **OpenID Foundation AuthZEN Working Group wins KuppingerCole EIC 2026 special award — EIC Berlin, 19-22 May 2026** — AuthZEN was recognised for creating a common, interoperable approach for authorisation decisions and modern policy services across legacy systems, home-grown apps, APIs, and AI processes (explicit list: MCP, A2A, LLM). AuthZEN 1.0 was ratified January 2026; the award marks 36 months of working-group output reaching production-readiness. Angle: a Tier-1 analyst event has just awarded the standard that externalises authorisation across human apps *and* AI agents — the policy decision-point layer that sits behind any "agentic identity" architecture. The 05-20 cidaas keynote (AI-driven SaaS as auth/authz/architecture problem) and the 05-21 carry-forward on DIF KYA-OS (Vouched's agentic-identity spec in 14-day review before vote) both pointed at this; AuthZEN is the standard that makes it shippable. The orchestration play is: identity orchestration (who is the principal) and AuthZEN-style policy decisions (what may that principal do, in which scope, on whose behalf) are two halves of the same architecture — and agentic delegation needs both.

- **Verifiable Legal Entity Identifier (vLEI) — cryptographic organisational identity for the AI-fraud era — Biometric Update, 21 May 2026** — Feature piece arguing that AI-driven document forgery (synthetic letters of authority, forged board resolutions, synthetic regulator notices) cannot be neutralised at the document layer alone; cross-border organisational trust needs to live at a cryptographic *entity-identity* layer. vLEI extends the ISO Legal Entity Identifier into a cryptographically verifiable organisational identity that can confirm both *the entity* behind a credential and *the authority of the individual* acting on its behalf. Hong Kong's Greater Bay Area (three legal systems, one trade corridor) cited as the live test case. In the same window, former GLEIF CEO Stephan Wolf joined LEI Worldwide's board to back vLEI growth. Significance for Ditto: this is the cross-border organisational-identity counterpart to Ditto's natural-person orchestration story — i.e. the architecture *companies* need so that a relying party can verify a credential was issued by the entity it claims, and that the human (or agent) presenting it actually has the authority. Pairs with the AuthZEN award (who's allowed to do what) and the agentic-identity standing theme (delegation must be scope-bound and revocable).
  - Source: https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/ai-generated-fraud-accelerates-push-for-cryptographic-organizational-identity
  - Date: 2026-05-21

### Pillar 3: Fraud / Deepfakes
- **EU AI Act Article 50 Code of Practice — July 22 signatory deadline now 29 days out, presumption of conformity at stake** — The European Commission published the final Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content on 10 June 2026 (press release IP/26/1328). Companies that submit their signatory form to the EU AI Office by **22 July 2026 (18:00 CEST)** are listed as initial signatories and gain a legal "presumption of regulatory conformity" with Article 50 obligations. Those that do not sign must independently demonstrate compliance to national market surveillance authorities once **2 August 2026** enforcement begins — a materially higher evidential bar. Obligations that become binding on 2 August: (i) machine-readable watermarking and metadata embedded in all AI-generated audio, image, and video outputs; (ii) explicit labelling of deepfakes — defined as AI content "that falsely appears authentic" of a real person; (iii) user disclosure where chatbots or virtual agents interact with humans. Penalties: up to €15 million or 3% of global annual turnover. The signing form is live on the EU AI Office's digital-strategy portal. Note: prior deltas (June 19 and June 22) flagged August 2 as a next-cycle anchor but did not surface the July 22 action deadline or the conformity-presumption benefit of signing. For Ditto: any customer deploying AI-generated content in regulated workflows, liveness/anti-spoofing tooling that produces synthetic-face references for comparison, or deepfake-detection products that must label detected content will be in scope. Identity vendors who sign the Code get a presumption of compliance; those who do not face a higher burden in any supervisory review.

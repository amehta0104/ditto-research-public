## Override-worthy this cycle

1. **Microsoft has documented a Windows 11 API that hands an application an age bracket and never a birthday — selective disclosure has stopped being a wallet feature and become an operating-system primitive.** `GetUserAgeRangeAsync` returns one of five buckets (under 10, 10–12, 13–15, 16–17, 18+) and explicitly does not return age, date of birth or family relationships; a companion call reports only whether the identity provider independently verified the age. Account: / company. Angle: every argument Ditto makes about proving a fact instead of shipping a file has just been implemented, by Microsoft, as a default in the world's most-installed desktop OS. The interesting part is not that it exists — it is that **an app which asks for a date of birth after this ships is now visibly asking for more than it needs**, and regulators will notice. Data minimisation stops being a principle you argue for and becomes a baseline you fall below.

2. **A pension fund paying 80,000 people across 190 countries runs face biometrics at scale and holds none of the biometric data — and says no fraud attempt has gone undetected.** The UN Joint Staff Pension Fund's Digital Certificate of Entitlement captures and stores the biometric only on the beneficiary's own device; the fund does not collect, process, store or transmit it, and the blockchain layer records that a verification happened without the personal or biometric detail. Account: company /. Angle: the standing objection to retain-nothing identity architecture is that it cannot work at scale, across borders, for non-technical users. This is 80,000 people, average age well above 70, many in their eighties and nineties, in 190 countries, running for five years. **It is the strongest available existence proof that the honeypot is a choice, not a requirement** — and it sits directly opposite the 153-million-licence-scan dump logged on 09-03.

3. **Liveness detection has stopped being a feature claim and become contested intellectual property — BASF is suing Apple over seven patents covering the skin-and-material detection behind Face ID's anti-spoofing.** Account:. Angle: for three years the category argued about whether liveness works. That argument is over; the new one is about who owns the layer that makes it work. When the anti-spoofing substrate becomes litigable IP, "we do liveness" stops being a datasheet line and starts being a question about what you actually own — which is a question a buyer can now reasonably ask in an RFP.

### Pillar 11: Age assurance & privacy attributes

- **Microsoft has documented Windows 11 APIs that give applications an age bracket and an age-verified flag, and explicitly never a date of birth — the operating system becomes the age oracle, and the app is designed out of the data-collection loop.** `GetUserAgeRangeAsync` returns one of five ranges — **under 10, 10–12, 13–15, 16–17, 18+** — as `Lower`/`Upper` integer pairs, or `null` when unknown, in which case apps must fall back to their own age gate. `GetAgeVerificationStatusAsync` returns **Verified, Unverified, OptedOut, TemporarilyUnavailable or NotApplicable**, reporting only whether the identity provider independently verified the age. Microsoft's stated privacy position: *"The API does not return the user's age, date of birth, or family relationship details."* The Digital Safety platform validates the calling app's identity before returning any signal. Requirements: Windows 11, Win32 COM, a signed-in Microsoft account, registered app identity. **The APIs are documented ahead of availability, are not enabled at runtime, and are "planned to be turned on in a future release later this year."** The regulatory driver is explicit — **California's Digital Age Assurance Act places a comparable duty on OS providers from 2027-01-01**, which the corpus has tracked as AB 1043 alongside the AB 1856 open-source exemption. **This closes the loop on the 09-01 delta's note that "Windows, macOS, iOS and Android remain in scope": one of the four has now published its implementation.**
  - Source (primary, Microsoft): https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/develop/security/age-signals/
  - Source: https://idtechwire.com/windows-11-will-hand-apps-an-age-bracket-instead-of-a-birthday/
  - Source: https://www.windowslatest.com/2026/09/03/windows-11-will-start-telling-apps-your-age-but-microsoft-says-it-wont-share-your-birthday/
  - Date: 2026-09-04 (trade coverage; Microsoft documentation dated 2026-08-27, APIs not yet enabled)
  - **⚠️ Three drafting constraints. (1) Write "documented", not "launched" — the APIs return `null` today. (2) This is not a zero-knowledge proof; Microsoft's identity provider holds the real age and asserts a range. Claiming otherwise is technically wrong and a knowledgeable reader will catch it. (3) The honest Ditto angle is the direction of travel — platforms are moving to attribute assertions, and the architecture that gets there without a trusted platform holding the underlying data is the one Ditto sells.**

- **The first US law targeting VPN use for age verification took effect on 2026-09-03, and the state agreed not to enforce it four days later.** Utah's **Senate Bill 73** requires adult sites to account for location-masking technology. The **Utah Department of Commerce** and **Aylo Freesites Ltd.** (Pornhub's parent) entered a stipulation pausing enforcement, extended on **2026-08-28** to run until **2026-10-22** or until the federal court rules on Aylo's preliminary-injunction request, whichever comes first. Aylo sued in **April 2026**, arguing there is *"no feasible way for a private company like Aylo to reliably verify whether any particular individual is using a virtual private network (VPN), proxy server, or other location-masking technology — and therefore no way to determine whether a user who appears to be located outside Utah is, in fact, located inside Utah."* **Judge David Barlow** has not ruled and told a hearing he could not commit to when he would. **This is the jurisdictional hole in every geography-gated age-assurance regime stated on the record by the defendant and not yet answered by the court** — and it is the same structural failure the Croatia alcohol law exposed on the tourist/OIB side. A credential the user carries proves what it proves regardless of the IP address it arrives from; a location-inferred obligation does not survive a VPN.

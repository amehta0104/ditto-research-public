### Pillar 3: Fraud / Deepfakes

- **Mayer Brown "AI Security and the Law" podcast: "Blurred Lines — How AI Is Rewriting the Rules of Fraud, Identity, and Defense"** — Mayer Brown partners Veronica Glick and Stephen Lilley interview Amy Larsen (former Visa / Microsoft Democracy Forward), covering: (i) how AI makes fraud faster, cheaper, more personalised and harder to detect; (ii) the blurring of attack categories (scam vs. cyberattack vs. disinformation) that breaks siloed regulatory frameworks; (iii) agentic AI and the specific liability gap it opens when an agent — not a human — initiates a transaction or identity claim; and (iv) the emerging US patchwork of state-level and federal responses. Key liability framing: current KYC and MFA rules assume a human explicitly authorising each step — agentic flows expose an unregulated seam that neither PSD3-style SCA nor existing US bank-secrecy rules were written for. Secondary analysis, but from a firm that advises regulated FIs and synthesises live client experience.

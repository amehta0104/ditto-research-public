### Pillar 3: Fraud / Deepfakes

- **TransUnion estimates US lenders face $2.4 billion in synthetic identity fraud exposure, and launches the first adverse-actionable fraud scoring products.** The estimates, drawn from TransUnion's own credit-file data as of June 30, 2026, find auto lending is the single largest exposure category at $1.5 billion.

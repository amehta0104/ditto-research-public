### Pillar 2: Identity ecosystem

- **Central Bank of Jordan selects BIO-key International for national biometric authentication initiative across Jordan's financial sector (July 28, 2026)** — The Central Bank of Jordan (CBJ) announced it is working with BIO-key (Nasdaq: BKYI) to deploy biometric-first authentication across the Jordanian financial ecosystem, covering a total population of 11.6 million. The stated objective is to reduce and ultimately eliminate end-client dependency on passwords, tokens, and traditional credentials, replacing them with biometric authentication tied directly to individual identity. Technology: BIO-key's PortalGuard and WEB-key platforms. BIO-key cites a prior large-scale deployment serving over 30 million customers. For Ditto: this is a central-bank-mandated, national-scale biometric authentication programme in a Middle Eastern market — sovereign mandation as the adoption model, not market pull. The framing (eliminate passwords, tie authentication to the individual) is directionally the same argument makes, now expressed as a central bank initiative outside the European regulatory sphere. Not an enterprise pilot; a national financial sector rollout.

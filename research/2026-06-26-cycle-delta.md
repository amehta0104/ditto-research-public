- **Shufti Deepfake Fraud Index (June 2026): document deepfakes +3,900%; synthetic ID = 42% of all AI fraud; live video deepfake only 17.6% of attacks** — Shufti annualised Jan–May 2026 fraud-attempt data and published its Deepfake Fraud Index in June 2026.

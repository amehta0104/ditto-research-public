### Pillar 1: Banking & Payments
- **KPMG 2026 Banking Technology Survey — banks signal a hard pivot to AI-enabled biometric identity defenses; deepfakes named a top-tier threat (published June 17, 2026; missed by prior cycles, surfaced in-window June 25)** — KPMG US released its 2026 Banking Technology Survey on **June 17, 2026** (missed by the June 17–19 delta cycles). Headline data points directly on Ditto's #1 pillar: only **32%** of banking leaders currently use AI-enabled biometrics to secure payments and access management, but **72%** plan to adopt the technology within three years — a 40-point intent gap that maps to the next cycle of identity-security procurement. **80%** expect AI to significantly disrupt their business/operating models in 3–5 years; **76%** saw an increase in cyberattacks in the past year; **92%** are raising cyber-risk budgets overall and **84%** are increasing cybersecurity investment *specifically* to counter AI-introduced risks. Top emerging threats ranked by execs: AI-introduced code vulnerabilities (**63%**), **deepfakes (62%)**, AI bots (57%), securing agentic technologies (50%). Tech is also now central to bank M&A — **77%** call it a primary or key driver of acquisition strategy over the next 2–3 years (KPMG US Banking Sector Leader Peter Torrente: "AI, payments modernization, cybersecurity, and tech-driven M&A are no longer separate agendas"). For Ditto: this is the demand-side counterpart to the regulatory mandates (BSP OTP sunset, MiCA, AMLA) the deltas have been tracking — the buyers themselves now rank deepfakes second and put AI-biometrics on a three-year clock. Strong banking-pillar post fuel; the deepfake-as-#2-threat stat is reusable for the fraud pillar.

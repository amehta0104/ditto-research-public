### Pillar 6: LATAM
- **Brazil Drex: Economic Development Committee approves Bill 4212/25, advancing anti-surveillance CBDC guardrails to Chamber floor vote (June 12, 2026)** — Brazil's lower-house Economic Development Committee approved a revised version of Bill 4212/25 on June 12, establishing that the Drex CBDC cannot replace paper money, cannot be mandated as legal tender, cannot be used for political or ideological surveillance, and that government access to transactions requires a court order. The bill now advances to floor votes in the full Chamber of Deputies before the Senate and presidential signature. The June 11 delta covered the first committee passage on June 9; this June 12 Economic Development Committee vote is the second distinct committee approval, confirming strong legislative momentum. Ditto significance: if enacted, Drex-integrated financial institutions would be structurally required to prove regulatory compliance without providing raw transaction data to the government — making ZKP-compatible KYC architecture a regulatory necessity, not just a differentiator.

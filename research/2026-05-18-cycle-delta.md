### Pillar 6: Identity verification (IDV)

- **OCR Studio ships anti-fraud detection for AI-generated and morphed identity documents — 17 May 2026** — OCR Studio updated its document-scanning tool with a system that detects identity documents generated or edited by tools such as ChatGPT, NanoBanana, Grok and Midjourney. The approach is the substantive part: it examines low-level image structure (generation/editing artifacts invisible to the human eye and undetectable by traditional logical-consistency anti-fraud) rather than checking for logical inconsistencies. CTO Konstantin Bulatov: "The only traces left by deepfake images are invisible to the human eye and undetectable by traditional anti-fraud systems." No named customers. Significance for Ditto: this is squarely Ditto Verify territory (document authenticity across 16,000+ ID types) and a category-level validation of the standing theme that document-only / logical-check KYC is structurally broken against generative-AI forgeries — the same gap the CBA $1bn underwriting-fraud case (baseline) and FATF Horizon Scan (baseline) describe. Use as category-pattern evidence, not vendor sniping (per competitors.md hard rule — do not name OCR Studio in a post).

### Pillar 3: EUDI / eIDAS2
- **EU and Japan validate cross-border digital wallet interoperability: EUDI Wallet ⇄ My Number Card credentials issued and verified across borders on open standards (Commission report July 16, coverage July 17, 2026)** — The European Commission (with Japan's Digital Agency) published a report on a pilot testing cross-border issuance and verification of credentials between a test **EU Digital Identity Wallet** and a Japanese wallet environment built on the **My Number Card** system. The pilot exchanged **academic credentials** (transcripts/enrolment) and explored real use cases including **bank-account opening and student exchange**, and confirmed that credentials can be "issued, stored, presented and verified across different countries and regions" using open standards despite differing technical and regulatory architectures. The report explicitly flags the remaining blocker as **mutual recognition of identity-management systems**, which "will require international agreements and aligned liability rules to create legally binding trust frameworks." Rooted in the EU–Japan Memorandum of Cooperation on Digital Identities and Trust Services (signed 30 Apr 2024). For Ditto: this is the first validated **EU–third-country** wallet interoperability result — directly relevant to the EUDI pillar (wallet-availability deadline 24 Dec 2026; relying-party acceptance in regulated sectors from ~late 2027) and a strong orchestration hook (P2), since the pilot's own conclusion is that the unsolved layer is trust-recognition/liability, not protocol.

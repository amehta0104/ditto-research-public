### Pillar 4: ZKPs in Practice

- **Sumsub + Chainlink: Cross-Chain Identity (CCID) for on-chain compliance — live 5 May 2026** — Users complete Sumsub's KYC flow and prove wallet ownership by signing a message; Chainlink's Automated Compliance Engine (ACE) issues a CCID — a reusable, privacy-preserving credential containing verified claims (e.g. "Age > 18," jurisdiction, AML-clear status). Credential is valid across Ethereum, Arbitrum, Avalanche, Polygon, and Base. No raw personal data ever hits the chain. Phase 2 (summer 2026) shifts to an asset-issuer model. Practical significance: first production deployment of portable KYC credentials across multiple Layer-1/Layer-2 chains using a major IDV vendor.

## Override-worthy this cycle

1. **Someone built a phone that lies to your KYC check — feeding a document to the rear camera and a face video to the front, at the operating-system level, where the verification app cannot see it.** Account: company /. Angle: this is the end of the "we do liveness detection" claim as a differentiator. If the camera itself is the adversary, every defence that lives inside the app is already behind the attacker. The four fixes the researchers recommend — server-generated fresh challenges, optical movement cross-checked against accelerometer and gyroscope, server-side attestation with fresh nonces, and treating attestation as *a* signal rather than proof — are an architecture, not a feature. Sharpest available line: **the honest version of liveness is not "is this face alive," it is "did this camera actually capture anything."**

2. **NIST and Europe's two largest telcos arrived at the same conclusion in the same week: agentic AI does not have an identity problem later, it has one now.** Account:. ### Pillar 6: Identity verification (IDV)

### Pillar 7: Identity ecosystem

- **FINRA mandates ID.me identity verification on its Entitlement Platform — phased rollout begins today, 11 May 2026** — SAAs prompted from today (rolling weekly through August), AAs from 29 June (through September), users with SSN/fingerprint access from 14 September (through October). ID.me selected on the basis of federal IAL2/AAL2 certification (NIST 800-63). No cost to FINRA users. Practical significance: a US self-regulatory organization with ~3,400 broker-dealer firms in scope just made reusable, federally-certified identity verification a precondition for back-office access — a much stronger signal than any single bank deployment. The story for an identity vendor: this is the first US regulated-infrastructure case where the IdV requirement is *not* the bank's choice; the regulator picked the vendor and the standard.

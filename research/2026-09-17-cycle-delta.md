### Pillar 2: EUDI / eIDAS 2

- **Moldova's eGovernance Agency launches EVO digital identity credentials for banking KYC — four commercial banks live as of September 16, 2026** — Moldova's state digital-identity app EVO is now accepted for customer identification at Moldindconbank, FinComBank, EuroCreditBank and Victoriabank. Customers scan a QR code generated by the bank, choose what data to share, and verified identity attributes are transferred digitally — no document photocopying or manual data entry. The eGovernance Agency opened EVO integration to public and private organisations on 2026-04-01; 30 relying parties are at various stages of connection, including Moldova's largest telco and the National Bank of Moldova; 20+ are targeted for operational integration by end-2026. EVO is built explicitly to the European Digital Identity Wallet framework and is designed for future cross-border use. Why this matters for an identity vendor: Moldova is a live, replicable example of a government-issued reusable credential accepted for a regulated financial use case — consent-based, attribute-selective, no raw document storage at the bank. It is the EUDI architecture in production, at smaller scale, 98 days before the EU's own December 24 wallet deadline.

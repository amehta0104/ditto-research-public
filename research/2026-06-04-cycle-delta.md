### Pillar 7: Identity ecosystem

- **Experian launches Agent Operating System™ — agentic AI trust layer for financial services (June 2, 2026).** — Experian announced the Agent Operating System™ (AOS) as a new "trusted agentic AI layer" within the Experian Ascend Platform, designed to let financial-services AI agents operate with verified identity, consent and fraud-risk signals. Key components: (1) **Agent Trust Token** — real-time signal encoding identity, consent and fraud risk for any AI-agent-initiated transaction; (2) **Agent Registry** — continuously evaluates agent behaviour over time; (3) **Human-to-Agent Binding** — connects verified consumers, devices and AI agents into a single cryptographic trust assertion. The framework uses a "Know Your Agent" (KYA) paradigm — directly echoing the DIF KYA-OS specification (v1.0 vote still pending), but as a proprietary financial-services product backed by Experian's ~1.4 billion consumer data relationships. Significance: The launch came on the same day as the Worldline/ING/Mastercard first-ever European production agentic payment (logged in the 06-03 delta). The pairing means (a) agentic payments are live on European rails and (b) a major incumbent is already staking the "who vouches for the agent?" trust layer with a proprietary token model — directly competing against open-standard approaches (DIF KYA-OS, FIDO Agentic Authentication TWG, Verifiable Credentials/ZKP delegation). For Ditto: the AOS frames the commercial threat sharply — credit-bureau-weight incumbents are commoditising credential delegation into a centralised token. Ditto's ZKP/split-key/selective-disclosure stack is the architectural counter-argument: cryptographic delegation without a proprietary oracle.

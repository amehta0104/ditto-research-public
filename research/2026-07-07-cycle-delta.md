### Pillar 3: EUDI / eIDAS2
- **EU eIDAS Dashboard evolving into the EUDI ecosystem's operational trust registry — now publishes machine- and human-readable trusted lists of PID providers, wallet providers, WRPAC (relying-party access certificate) providers, registrars/registers and (newly) age-verification services; Catalogue of Attributes, Schemes registry and EAA providers in development; Third-Countries AdES trusted-list already covers Moldova & Ukraine (Biometric Update / ID Tech, July 6, 2026)** — The European Commission's eIDAS Dashboard, described as "a key piece in the overall European Digital Identity Trust Framework," is being built out from a status tracker into the ecosystem's live registry of trusted entities. The operational **Age Verification Trusted List** is already published; under active development are the Catalogue of Attributes, the Schemes registry, and the Electronic Attestations of Attributes (EAA) provider registry. For Ditto: this is the *acceptance/orchestration substrate* of EUDI materialising ahead of the December 6, 2026 wallet-availability deadline — the register that determines which relying parties, attribute providers and age-verification services are trusted. Readiness is about being on these lists and orchestrating against them, not just supporting the wallet format. See override #1. Cross-listed P11 (age-verification trusted list).
